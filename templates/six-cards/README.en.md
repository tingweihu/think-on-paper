Give each candidate idea its own card before comparing or ordering them. Geometry change: separate cards: 6.

## When to use it

**Geometry relationship:** `parameter` · [four-cards](../four-cards/README.en.md)

- Reserve one summary card for each of six interviewees so common points can be revisited.
- Summarize six concepts after a chapter, placing one definition and short example in each cell.

## How to use it

1. Decide whether cards hold options or facets of one topic; use one comparable unit per card.
2. Use the worked example to place entries in region order: 1: "Library, ten seats"; 2: "Four short books"; 3: "Invite by Friday"; 4: "Saturday afternoon"; 5: "Two facilitators"; 6: "Collect feedback"
3. Review the regions, mark open questions and choose a next step.

## What the page helps you see

Separate units that can be reordered, compared, or selected.


## Worked example

Entries in example-position order; each number identifies one entry:

1. Library, ten seats
2. Four short books
3. Invite by Friday
4. Saturday afternoon
5. Two facilitators
6. Collect feedback

## When not to use it

Each of the six cards holds a summary, while card position conveys neither chronology nor causation; full interview transcripts and links between cards need separate space.
