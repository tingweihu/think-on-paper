Start with what you need to do, then open a format page for limits, worked examples, and blank downloads. These are starting points; the full catalog has every verified format.

## Record continuously

- [Ruled notes](../../templates/ruled-standard/README.en.md) — Record continuous sentences at a steady line spacing while keeping the page open to free writing. Limit: Continuous lines are awkward when several options need side-by-side comparison or when the relationships between causes matter.
- [Dot grid](../../templates/dot-standard/README.en.md) — Use faint dots for alignment while leaving writing more open than a full square grid. Limit: Dots can be too subtle when a clear cell boundary or continuous writing baseline is needed.
- [Equal two columns](../../templates/two-equal-columns/README.en.md) — Place two equally important streams side by side for rowwise or whole-page comparison. Limit: Equal width wastes room when one side holds only short cues and the other needs long notes.
- [Blank page](../../templates/blank/README.en.md) — Leave every mark and spatial relationship to the writer or sketcher. Limit: The page gives too little guidance for consistent ruled writing, precise tables, or shared forms with fixed fields.

## Expand and group ideas

- [Six-branch concept map](../../templates/mindmap-six/README.en.md) — Expand one central topic into six distinct angles to see the breadth of an idea. Limit: Outer circles hold short phrases; use a tree or columns for layered reasoning or long notes.
- [3×3 idea expansion](../../templates/mandala-3x3/README.en.md) — Put one question in the center and explore eight distinct aspects around it. Limit: Equal small cells are limiting when each aspect needs a long paragraph or when a strict sequence matters.
- [Cluster ring](../../templates/cluster-ring/README.en.md) — Gather loose material into four clusters before naming their shared theme. Limit: Four groups are a provisional aid, not a statistical classification or the only valid taxonomy.
- [Eight-sector classification wheel](../../templates/classification-wheel/README.en.md) — Place up to eight categories around one subject to notice gaps and overlap. Limit: Narrow sectors fit brief terms; eight is a layout capacity, not a required number of categories.

## Compare and choose

- [Four-quadrant notes](../../templates/quadrant/README.en.md) — Sort ideas into four areas defined by two dimensions you choose, then inspect the pattern. Limit: Many interdependent conditions or decisions requiring precise weights cannot be resolved by four areas alone.
- [Two-circle Venn diagram](../../templates/venn-two/README.en.md) — Separate features belonging to either group from those shared by both. Limit: Circle area is not a statistical scale; exact counts or many intersections need another representation.
- [Two-by-three matrix](../../templates/matrix-2x3/README.en.md) — Use six adjoining cells for the intersection of two criteria and three options. Limit: Cells are too narrow for paragraphs; numerical scores need stated units.
- [Two-set link map](../../templates/bipartite-network/README.en.md) — Compare many-to-many relationships between two kinds of element, with no within-set links. Limit: Use a general network for within-set relations; crossing lines do not imply conflict.

## Study and retrieve

- [Cornell notes](../../templates/cornell/README.en.md) — Separate recall cues, detailed notes, and a closing summary so the page can be reviewed actively. Limit: A large drawing or continuous mathematical derivation may be cramped by the narrow cue column.
- [Three question-answer pairs](../../templates/question-answer-triples/README.en.md) — Keep three checkable questions beside space for fuller answers. Limit: Three fixed pairs are poor for an open-ended discussion; unverified answers should not be treated as facts.
- [Concept, text, and diagram](../../templates/concept-text-diagram/README.en.md) — Pair a written explanation with a sketch under one concept and check that the two agree. Limit: An unsupported sketch is not a factual model; use a formal chart for measured proportions.
- [Concept and recall area](../../templates/concept-recall/README.en.md) — Keep a new concept and notes separate from a final attempt to recall it in your own words. Limit: The bottom holds a brief summary, not proof of long-term retention; do not treat a mistaken recall as the source's wording.

## Read sources and check claims

- [Excerpt above, thoughts below](../../templates/top-excerpt-bottom-thoughts/README.en.md) — Keep a quotation or observation above and personal questions and interpretations below. Limit: The upper area is not a complete citation system; long quotations and reuse rights still need separate care.
- [One claim, four evidence areas](../../templates/claim-four-evidence/README.en.md) — Separate a testable claim from four supporting, opposing, or missing pieces of information. Limit: Four cards are not a formal literature review, and fictional examples are not research results.
- [Linked source notes](../../templates/source-network-notes/README.en.md) — Compare notes from three sources and flag observations supported across sources. Limit: Cross-links do not prove source independence; full references and page numbers still belong in a proper citation record.
- [Hypothesis and three evidence cards](../../templates/hypothesis-evidence/README.en.md) — State a testable hypothesis, examine three observations or counterexamples, and update the assessment. Limit: Three cards cannot assess a complex study, and illustrative data are not experimental results.

## Examine causes and plan flows

- [Fishbone diagram](../../templates/fishbone/README.en.md) — Work backward from an observed outcome to possible causes instead of settling on the first explanation. Limit: The diagram organizes hypotheses; without evidence, its lines cannot establish that a cause is real.
- [Cross-linked cause network](../../templates/causal-network/README.en.md) — Map several possible factors and their cross-influences instead of forcing one cause chain. Limit: Lines can make guesses look certain; without evidence this remains a map of hypotheses.
- [Four-step horizontal flow](../../templates/flow-four-horizontal/README.en.md) — Show one process as four actions from left to right. Limit: A single path cannot fully describe branching, loops, or several responsible roles.
- [Two-level decision tree](../../templates/decision-tree/README.en.md) — Branch one question into two checks and four possible actions. Limit: The tree does not calculate probabilities or weights; too many conditions also crowd an A5 page.
- [Three-lane handoff flow](../../templates/swimlane-flow/README.en.md) — Separate three roles into horizontal lanes and follow their handoffs with arrows. Limit: Several parallel branches at once need a more detailed process map.

## Sketch scenes and options

- [Six-panel storyboard](../../templates/storyboard-6/README.en.md) — Sketch a sequence in six frames and add one short action note beneath each image. Limit: A long sequence or production that needs exact shot timing requires more frames and a fuller specification.
- [Mixed-scale eight-panel storyboard](../../templates/storyboard-eight-mixed/README.en.md) — Use broad frames for scenes, four small beats for action, and broad frames for the close. Limit: Small panels cannot carry detail or long prose; production still needs timing, sound, and camera planning.
- [Three sketch proposals](../../templates/three-sketch-proposals/README.en.md) — Draw three distinct approaches and keep a short evaluation beside each. Limit: A sketch is not a measured plan or user test, and the narrow strip cannot hold a full argument.
- [Main and detail sketch](../../templates/detail-sketch/README.en.md) — Draw an overall idea and enlarge one area that needs discussion, with notes alongside. Limit: The inset signals focus, not a measured enlargement ratio or a construction drawing.
- [Two-point perspective guides](../../templates/perspective-guides/README.en.md) — Use a horizon and two vanishing points to draft rough two-point perspective. Limit: Printed guides show one provisional view, not photogrammetry or a measured architectural perspective.

## Use special writing paper

- [Blank music staff](../../templates/music-staff/README.en.md) — Six five-line staves provide room for handwritten pitch, rhythm, and short melody drafts. Limit: No clef, key, or time signature is printed; beginners need a reliable reference. Fine-line print clarity remains untested.
- [Six-line guitar tablature](../../templates/guitar-tablature/README.en.md) — Use six string lines to jot fret positions and short playing patterns. Limit: Tablature does not automatically carry full rhythm, tone, or fingering, and alternate tunings change pitches. Fine-line print is untested.
- [Tian character practice grid](../../templates/hanzi-tian/README.en.md) — Use a vertical and horizontal center guide to inspect balance within one character square. Limit: The cross is a spatial guide, not a typeface standard or stroke-order lesson. Each A5 cell is about 21 mm and is not for long prose.
- [Nine-section character grid](../../templates/hanzi-nine/README.en.md) — Divide each character square into nine guide regions to inspect component placement. Limit: Each interior section is about 7 mm and is not a separate writing cell. Fine-line print clarity remains untested.
- [Seyès-style ruled draft](../../templates/seyes-ruled/README.en.md) — Use closely spaced guides to practice letter height and baseline placement. Limit: This is a provisional Seyès-style draft, not certified to a school standard. Two-millimetre guide print quality is untested.

## Combine drawing and notes

- [Dot field and open field](../../templates/dot-and-blank/README.en.md) — Use dots for spatial planning above and leave an open area below for free notes. Limit: Dots are not a calibrated ruler; do not use this as a construction plan without measured dimensions.
- [Concept map with ruled notes](../../templates/mindmap-and-ruled/README.en.md) — Explore four directions above, then explain one in readable prose below. Limit: Small circles fit short terms; use a full concept map when many branches matter.
- [Mixed-texture quadrants](../../templates/mixed-quadrant/README.en.md) — Combine ruled, dotted, and open regions so prose and sketches can share one four-part page. Limit: Different textures do not guarantee comparable information; use plain quadrants for equal-density regions.
- [Three cards, main sketch, conclusion](../../templates/hybrid-multi-zone/README.en.md) — List three conditions, draw one overall idea, and state a conclusion supported by the sketch. Limit: A sketch is not a validated solution, and a few bottom lines cannot hold a full research record.
