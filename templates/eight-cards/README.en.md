Give each candidate idea its own card before comparing or ordering them. Geometry change: separate cards: 8.

## When to use it

**Geometry relationship:** `parameter` · [four-cards](../four-cards/README.en.md)

- Draft descriptions for eight exhibits on eight equally sized cards.
- Keep eight independently addressable topics separate before deciding their order.

## How to use it

1. Decide whether cards hold options or facets of one topic; use one comparable unit per card.
2. Use the worked example to place entries in region order: 1: "Library, ten seats"; 2: "Four short books"; 3: "Invite by Friday"; 4: "Saturday afternoon"; 5: "Two facilitators"; 6: "Check budget"; 7: "Clear passage"; 8: "Collect feedback"
3. Review the regions, mark open questions and choose a next step.

## What the page helps you see

Separate units that can be reordered, compared, or selected.


## Worked example

Entries in example-position order; each number identifies one entry:

## When not to use it

Eight equal areas do not establish hierarchy or priority; forcing entries of very different lengths into equal cards can crowd some of them.
