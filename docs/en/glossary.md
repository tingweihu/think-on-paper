| Cornell notes | 康乃爾式筆記頁 | コーネル式ノート | This printable page has cue, main-note, and summary zones; the Chinese label distinguishes the page from the wider note-taking method. No claim of measured learning benefit. |

| storyboard | 故事板 | 絵コンテ | Sequential scenes, events, or shot sketches. |
