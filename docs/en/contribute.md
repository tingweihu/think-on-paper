# Contribute

[Home](../../README.en.md) · [繁體中文](../zh-TW/contribute.md) · [日本語](../ja/contribute.md)

Check [`catalog-144.csv`](../../catalog-144.csv), the trilingual catalog, and existing template manifests before proposing a format. Explain how its partition structure or writing behavior differs. A new line spacing, orientation, or paper color is a variant of an existing format. Add a builder, stable ID, purpose and limits in three languages, actionable steps, and worked examples on the same scene. Generate the files and run QA. Blank sheets cannot contain words, page numbers, dates, or logos.

Translation improvements should keep the limitations, example, and print advice. State whether a human reviewed the text; an unreviewed digital preview must not claim that review passed. For a print problem, report scaling settings, paper, and measurements without including private notes. Original project code, documentation, examples, and artwork are under [MIT](../../LICENSE).

For every code, text, or drawing contribution, identify its author and source and confirm you have the right to submit it under MIT. Do not add third-party images, fonts, trademarks, or someone else's notes. External contributions still need source and rights review.
