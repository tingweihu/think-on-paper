# Code of Conduct

The project aims to make note formats usable by people with different languages, abilities, and writing habits. In discussions and contributions, be respectful, describe problems with reproducible evidence, and do not disclose another person's private notes or personal information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may ask for edits, hide unsafe material, or decline contributions that break these expectations. Sensitive concerns should not be posted publicly; no private reporting address is claimed until one is configured.
